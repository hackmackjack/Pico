# 🗺️ "Pico 500" Initiative: Project Roadmap

**Objective:** Create 500 distinct, educational projects for Grades 6-9 using the Pico V5.0 Ultimate IDE.
**Philosophy:** "From Blinking LED to Artificial Intelligence".

## 📚 The 8 Domains (Tracks)
We will divide the 500 projects into 8 major domains to ensure comprehensive coverage of the toolbox.

| # | Domain | Focus | Project Range | Target Blocks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Basics & Beginner** | Logic, Loops, Math, Variables | 001 - 060 | Logic, Loops, Math, GPIO Write, Wait |
| **2** | **Smart Sensors** | Environmental Sensing, Inputs | 061 - 130 | Sensor Read, Distance, ADC, Debounce |
| **3** | **Actuators & Automation** | Motion, Sound, Power Control | 131 - 190 | Servo, Motor, PWM, Relay (GPIO), Music |
| **4** | **Displays & Visuals** | OLED, NeoPixel, LCD | 191 - 250 | OLED Draw, Graph, NeoPixel Effects |
| **5** | **IoT & Connectivity** | WiFi, MQTT, Web Control | 251 - 320 | WiFi, HTTP, MQTT, Web Server, BLE |
| **6** | **Robotics** | Mobile Robots, Arms, Encoders | 321 - 390 | Drive Dist, Smart Servo, Encoders, PID |
| **7** | **Data & Science** | Logging, Graphing, Analysis | 391 - 440 | Files, Data Log, RTC, Statistics, Dicts |
| **8** | **AI & Advanced Games** | Machine Learning, Complex Logic | 441 - 500 | KNN, Gesture, Game Sprites, State Machines |

---

## 📅 Batch Structure (Agile Content Creation)
We will generate content in **Batches of 10 Projects**. Each batch focuses on a specific sub-theme.

### **Example: Domain 1 (Basics)**
*   **Batch 1 (001-010):** "Hello World" (LEDs & Printing).
*   **Batch 2 (011-020):** "Traffic Lights" (Timing & Sequences).
*   **Batch 3 (021-030):** "Buttons & Switches" (Input Logic).
*   **Batch 4 (031-040):** "Math Magic" (Variables & Calculation).
*   **Batch 5 (041-050):** "The Looper" (Advanced Loops).
*   **Batch 6 (051-060):** "Reaction Games" (Combining Inputs + Time).

## 📝 Proposal: Batch 1 (Projects 001-010)
**Theme:** "Hello World & Digital Output"
**Goal:** Master the `set Pin` and `wait` blocks.

1.  **P001: Hello World** - Print "Hello" to Console.
2.  **P002: The Blinking LED** - Blink the onboard LED (Pin 25/LED).
3.  **P003: Double Blink** - Create a "Heartbeat" pattern (Blink-Blink-Wait).
4.  **P004: SOS Signal** - Morse Code (3 Short, 3 Long, 3 Short).
5.  **P005: Police Strobe** - Alternate two external LEDs (Red/Blue).
6.  **P006: Traffic Light Sim** - Red -> Yellow -> Green Sequence.
7.  **P007: Random Sparkle** - Randomly flash an LED (intro to Math Random).
8.  **P008: Running Lights** - Chase effect with 3 LEDs.
9.  **P009: Binary Counter** - Count 0-7 using 3 LEDs (Intro to binary concepts).
10. **P010: The Unreliable Light** - Blink with random delays (Is it broken? No, it's code!).
